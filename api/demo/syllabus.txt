GAPFINDER DEMO SYLLABUS - CLASS 10 SCIENCE
This syllabus was written for the GapFinder demo workspace. It is a sample, not an official board syllabus.

# Unit 1: Life Processes
## Photosynthesis
Raw materials of photosynthesis, the role of chlorophyll and sunlight, and its products.
## Stomata and gas exchange
Gas exchange through stomata and the opening and closing of guard cells.
## Respiration
Aerobic and anaerobic respiration and the energy each releases.
## Transportation in humans
The four-chambered heart, arteries, veins and capillaries, and the components of blood.

# Unit 2: Electricity
## Current and potential difference
Electric current, electric charge and potential difference, with their SI units.
## Ohm's law and resistance
Ohm's law and its graph; resistance and the factors it depends on, including resistivity.
## Resistors in series and parallel
Combinations of resistors in series and in parallel.
## Heating effect and electric power
Heating effect of electric current, electric power and commercial energy units.

# Unit 3: Light - Reflection and Refraction
## Reflection and mirrors
Laws of reflection; image formation by plane, concave and convex mirrors.
## Refraction and refractive index
Refraction of light, refractive index and Snell's law.
## Lenses
Image formation by convex and concave lenses; the lens formula, magnification and power of a lens.

Assessment: one practice paper and two term examinations.
